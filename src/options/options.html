Blipr

Watch a page for an element and get a blip on your phone the moment it shows up, or the moment the last one goes away.

Create the topic in the Blipr app first. Publishing to a topic that does not exist yet is refused by the server.

Watches

Add a watch

A tab you are not looking at is throttled, and many sites stop updating themselves while hidden. Reloading the page on a timer gives the watch a fresh page anyway. Reloading also discards anything unsaved on that page, and Blipr never reloads the tab you are looking at. Once an interval is set you can start and stop the reloads from the watch itself.
